13How this works

Best lap. For every driver we take the single fastest non-pit-out lap of the session you selected — nothing else from the weekend is used.

Same point on track. Speed samples arrive over time, but drivers reach each point at different times. We integrate speed into distance travelled and resample every lap to 1,000 evenly spaced points, so point 500 is the same piece of asphalt for everyone.

Corners. Apex positions come from official circuit data fetched live. Each corner is a small window (±1.2% of lap distance) around its apex.

Ranking. Within each window we average each driver's speed. Highest average wins the corner. A team's score is the mean of its drivers. "Corners won" counts windows topped.

Tyres & sectors. Compounds come from the session's stint list matched to the lap number of each best lap; sectors and traps are the official splits of that same lap.
